Data boundary

The on-screen brief, run-sheet preview, print output, and CSV contain meeting setup and task information and must be protected as business data. They exclude discussion notes, decisions, summaries, consent details, typed signatures, attachments, credentials, private keys, provider secrets, synchronization queues, and hidden governance metadata. A same-day warning identifies shared calendar dates; it is not proof that meeting times overlap.
