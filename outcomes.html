Protected business-data boundary

The screen and CSV contain meeting identity plus aggregate decision and task state. Protect them as business data. They exclude raw record identifiers, attendee information, signatures, consent, discussion notes, summary text, free-form decision text, structured decision text and conditions, task text, Assigned To values, credentials, private keys, provider secrets, synchronization payloads, and hidden governance metadata.

Decision and task counts belong to the same source meeting. The review never claims that a task implements, proves, or is linked to a specific decision.
