# Methodz Meeting Manager v1.6.10 Field Rehearsal

## Purpose

This is the release-blocking physical-device rehearsal for the static, offline-first Methodz Meeting Manager. It validates direct meeting work, service-worker behavior, printing, cross-device transfer acceptance, and rollback on real hardware.

The rehearsal creates metadata-only operator evidence. It does not authenticate a person or device and does not prove delivery, legal approval, or production-provider durability.

## Required matrix

Complete every row before marking v1.6.10 field-ready.

| Platform | Required environment | Core meeting workflow | Offline reload | Print/PDF | Transfer acceptance | Rollback |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| iPhone | Current Safari, HTTPS | Required | Required | Required | Required | Required |
| Tablet | Android tablet or iPad | Required | Required | Required | Required | Required |
| Desktop | Chromium plus Firefox or Safari/WebKit | Required | Required | Required | Required | Required |

Use disposable meeting records only.

## Preparation

1. Record the app-shell commit SHA and device/browser versions.
2. Export a complete protected workspace backup from every browser profile involved.
3. Keep private signing keys separate from workspace packages.
4. Confirm the deployment is not `hub.methodz.ca`.
5. Confirm the service worker is used only on HTTPS or localhost.
6. Open `docs/field-rehearsal-evidence-template.json` and make one copy per device/browser profile.

## Core meeting workflow

On every device:

1. Open `meeting.html`.
2. Confirm the Panel Registry Diagnostics panel remains hidden when validation passes.
3. Enter Meeting-Day Mode.
4. Create a disposable meeting with title, date, location, facilitator, two organizations, two attendees, agenda selections, discussion notes, one structured decision, two Assigned To tasks, and a summary.
5. Save the record.
6. Reload and reopen it.
7. Update one field and confirm revision history is created.
8. Archive and restore the record.
9. Export TXT and JSON.
10. Confirm no page-level horizontal overflow and that navigation remains touch friendly.

Acceptance criteria:

- all registered capture panels remain reachable;
- Meeting-Day navigation works after headings are localized or changed;
- save, edit, revision, archive, and restore preserve the disposable record;
- supporting tools remain explicitly available;
- a registry failure is visible and never hides meeting controls.

## Service-worker registration and offline reload

On HTTPS or localhost:

1. Open the application while online.
2. Confirm the active cache is `methodz-meeting-manager-v1.6.10`.
3. Confirm the service worker reaches the activated state.
4. Close the tab.
5. Disable network connectivity.
6. Reopen the installed app or URL.
7. Open the meeting workspace, archive entry point, and verifier entry point.
8. Confirm existing browser-local records remain readable.
9. Re-enable connectivity and confirm the shell refreshes normally.

Acceptance criteria:

- static application assets load offline after one successful online visit;
- no meeting record, queue, transfer, acceptance, rollback, or private-key value appears in Cache Storage;
- no background sync event processes application work;
- direct-file core operation remains usable without service-worker registration.

## Print and PDF

On every platform with print support:

1. Open a disposable saved record in `archive.html`.
2. Print or save as PDF.
3. Confirm meeting information, attendance, agenda, decisions, tasks, attachment references, summary, and audit metadata are legible.
4. Confirm buttons, provider panels, synchronization panels, transfer panels, acceptance panels, diagnostics, and Meeting-Day controls are absent.
5. Confirm no horizontal clipping or blank trailing pages caused by hidden infrastructure panels.

## Transfer acceptance and rollback

Use separate source and destination browser profiles, preferably on separate physical devices.

### Source

1. Save one active disposable meeting and one archived disposable meeting.
2. Add one revision, directory entry, template, governance record, public verification-key record, custody event, recovery event, and queued rehearsal item.
3. Run Device Readiness.
4. Build and download the v1.6.8 transfer bundle.
5. Store it outside the browser.
6. Leave the source workspace unchanged.

### Destination

1. Create a distinct disposable destination record.
2. Export a destination backup.
3. Run Device Readiness.
4. Inspect the transfer bundle.
5. Run the no-write recovery drill.
6. Review collisions and replacement counts.
7. Complete the typed `TRANSFER` approval.
8. Reload after verified import.
9. Complete v1.6.9 destination acceptance.
10. Export the metadata-only acceptance report.
11. Preview rollback.
12. Preserve the transferred state as the pre-rollback recovery package.
13. Complete the typed `ROLLBACK` approval.
14. Reload and confirm the original destination record returns.
15. Confirm the pre-rollback package can reproduce the transferred disposable state.

Acceptance criteria:

- source and destination remain distinguishable;
- no silent import, acceptance, or rollback occurs;
- checksums and component validation pass;
- pre-import and pre-rollback packages exist before replacement operations;
- destination acceptance contains aggregate metadata only;
- rollback restores the original destination state;
- failure injection restores the transferred snapshot;
- reports contain no meeting values, raw record IDs, attendee names, signatures, credentials, private keys, or storage-key names.

## Evidence handling

Populate one JSON evidence file per platform using `docs/field-rehearsal-evidence-template.json`.

Allowed evidence:

- platform and browser family/version;
- viewport class;
- app-shell commit SHA;
- pass, fail, blocked, or not-applicable outcomes;
- aggregate duration and count values;
- operator notes that contain no meeting content;
- screenshot or trace references stored outside the meeting workspace.

Do not include:

- meeting titles or notes;
- attendee or customer names;
- record IDs;
- signatures;
- credentials or tokens;
- private keys;
- raw localStorage keys or values;
- complete transfer or recovery packages.

## Release decision

v1.6.10 is field-ready only when:

- the full platform matrix is complete;
- every required row passes or has a documented blocking defect;
- blocking defects are linked to GitHub issues;
- Panel Registry Diagnostics is valid on every tested shell;
- the two-device transfer, acceptance, and rollback rehearsal passes;
- protected backups remain available until the release is accepted.
