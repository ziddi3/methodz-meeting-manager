# Methodz Meeting Manager v1.6.9 Transfer Acceptance

## Purpose

Version 1.6.9 turns the v1.6.8 cross-device transfer machinery into a complete destination-acceptance workflow without introducing a backend, credentials, silent synchronization, or a meeting-record schema change.

The destination operator can now:

1. verify that a v1.6.8 destination import completed;
2. review aggregate counts for every required workspace component;
3. record metadata-only acceptance evidence;
4. run aggregate workspace diagnostics;
5. preview restoration of the locally stored pre-import recovery package;
6. preserve the current destination as a separate rollback-recovery package;
7. restore and read back the pre-import snapshot only after explicit approval.

## Acceptance checklist

The checklist covers:

- active meeting records;
- Archive Vault records;
- revision history;
- attendee and organization directories;
- meeting templates;
- governance, retention, release, approval, policy, receipt, and disposition metadata;
- public verification keys;
- key-custody, rotation, revocation, and lost-key records;
- recovery packages and drill logs;
- tenant-scoped synchronization queue state.

Each row reports only aggregate item counts, storage-entry counts, and byte estimates. The report excludes meeting content, raw record identifiers, attendee names, signatures, credentials, and private-key material.

Acceptance is blocked unless the v1.6.8 transfer state is `destination-import-verified` and every checklist item is explicitly reviewed.

## Rollback rehearsal

The rollback path uses the pre-import recovery package created by the v1.6.8 transfer workflow.

Before any replacement:

- the recovery package is parsed and checksum-verified;
- the restore plan is calculated without writing data;
- add, replace, unchanged, remove, and ignored counts are shown;
- the destination fingerprint is captured;
- the operator must review the plan and type `ROLLBACK`;
- a final browser confirmation is required.

Immediately before restoration, the current destination workspace is exported into `methodzTransferRollbackRecoveryV169`. The verified pre-import entries are then written and read back. If the write cannot be verified, the original destination entries are restored automatically and verified.

The rollback report contains only stage, verification flags, operation counts, versions, timestamp, and checksum.

## Large-workspace diagnostics

Diagnostics measure:

- recognized workspace-entry count;
- aggregate UTF-8 byte size;
- parseable and unparseable storage-entry counts;
- component-level aggregate counts and bytes;
- browser storage usage and quota when the browser exposes them;
- persistent-storage status;
- scan duration;
- bounded warning codes.

Diagnostics never include storage values, storage-key names, meeting content, raw identifiers, credentials, or private keys.

## Operator sequence

### Accept a destination

1. Complete the v1.6.8 destination transfer and read-back verification.
2. Open **Transfer Acceptance & Rollback**.
3. Press **Refresh Review**.
4. Compare the aggregate counts with the expected source workspace.
5. Review every checklist row.
6. Press **Complete Acceptance**.
7. Confirm the metadata-only evidence write.
8. Download the acceptance report and a fresh workspace backup.
9. Keep the source workspace unchanged until the destination has been used successfully.

### Rehearse rollback

1. Press **Preview Rollback**.
2. Confirm that the pre-import package checksum verifies.
3. Review the replacement counts.
4. Check the review confirmation.
5. Type `ROLLBACK`.
6. Press **Restore Pre-Import Recovery**.
7. Confirm the final dialog.
8. Verify the success message.
9. Download the rollback report.
10. Reload the restored workspace when ready.

## Boundaries

Browser-local acceptance evidence does not authenticate a person or device. It does not prove authority, delivery, legal approval, or remote audit. The service worker caches only static files and never reads or processes workspace, transfer, acceptance, rollback, diagnostics, or queue data.
