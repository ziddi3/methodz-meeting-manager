# Methodz Meeting Manager v1.6.9 Changelog

## Added

- Guided post-transfer acceptance across active records, Archive Vault records, revisions, directories, templates, governance metadata, public verification keys, custody records, recovery logs, and tenant queue state.
- Expected-count comparisons against the latest verified v1.6.8 destination-import report.
- Explicit operator acceptance with per-category review, recovery-retention confirmation, and typed `ACCEPT` approval.
- Metadata-only acceptance reports with bounded browser-local retention.
- No-write preview and explicit restoration of the pre-import destination snapshot.
- Pre-rollback preservation of the current transferred workspace.
- Verified rollback writes, required removals, failure recovery, and metadata-only rollback evidence.
- Compact Meeting-Day Mode with section navigation, phone/tablet support, supporting-panel expansion, preference restoration, and `Alt+M` shortcut.
- Aggregate large-workspace diagnostics for bytes, entry counts, parse health, size buckets, quota ratio, and scan duration.
- Browser and Node regression coverage.
- v1.6.9 architecture, operator workflow, and test documentation.

## Changed

- App-shell and service-worker cache version advance to `1.6.9`.
- Record schema remains `1.6.0`.
- `meeting.html` loads `config-v169.js`, `transfer-acceptance-core.js`, `features-v169-transfer-acceptance.js`, `features-v169-meeting-day.js`, and `features-v169.css`.
- The direct meeting workflow is now easier to isolate from governance and infrastructure panels during a live meeting.

## Safety boundaries

- No production provider, endpoint, credential, authenticated account, or automatic synchronization is introduced.
- Acceptance and rollback remain explicit operator actions.
- Service workers cache static assets only.
- Acceptance, rollback, and diagnostics reports exclude meeting content, raw record IDs, attendee names, signatures, credentials, private-key material, and storage-key names.
- Browser-local evidence does not authenticate a person or device and does not prove delivery, authority, or legal approval.
- The repository remains a task-focused meeting tool and must not deploy over `hub.methodz.ca`.
