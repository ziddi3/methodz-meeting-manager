Protected business-data boundary

The on-screen register and CSV may contain structured decision text, approval or confirmation labels, decision dates, and decision conditions. Protect them as business data. They exclude attendee data, signatures, consent details, discussion notes, tasks, summaries, credentials, private keys, provider secrets, synchronization queues, raw record identifiers, and hidden governance metadata. Free-form decision prose is not copied into this register or its CSV.
