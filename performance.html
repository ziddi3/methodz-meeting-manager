Evidence boundary

  • Only existing `methodz-workspace-capacity-rehearsal` version 1.0.0 metadata reports are accepted.
  • Privacy-boundary flags must match the established metadata-only contract or the report is rejected.
  • Unknown properties are ignored instead of copied into the comparison.
  • Imported reports are kept in memory only and are never written to browser storage.
  • No meeting content, record identifiers, raw storage keys or values, signatures, credentials, private keys, queue payloads, or transfer contents are emitted.
  • No provider call, synchronization, transfer, rollback, cleanup, or meeting mutation is performed.
